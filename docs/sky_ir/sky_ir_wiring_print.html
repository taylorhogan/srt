ESP32-S3 header, 44-pin DevKitC-1

USB-C ports at the bottom, component side up. Everything this build uses is in the left column: 3V3 is the top two pins, 8 is 12th from the top, 9 is 15th, 10 is 16th, 5V and GND are the last two. GPIO 22 to 25 do not exist on the S3. The board's silkscreen is the authority.

Notes

  • These are the ESP32-S3 pins (DevKitC-1, N16R8). GPIO 22 to 25 do not exist on the S3; on a classic ESP32-DevKitC the same wiring is SDA 21, SCL 22, heater 25. Pins 26 to 37 are the S3 module's flash and PSRAM, 19/20 are USB, 0/3/45/46 are strapping pins: leave them all alone.
  • The MLX runs at 3 V; never put its VDD or SDA/SCL on the 5 V rail.
  • Keep the I²C leads under 15 cm. The can sits in the lid, the board underneath.
  • One ground. The heater's 0.1 A returns through the same GND as the sensors; keep that lead short so the drop stays off the I²C ground reference.
  • Nothing is earthed: the USB adapter is isolated, so the circuit ground floats. Do not bond it to the mast or enclosure.
  • Solder hook-up wire straight to the BME280 and the MLX legs; use female headers on the perfboard only for the ESP32, USB connector toward the gland.
  • The MLX reports the sky and its own die; use the BME280 for the air, the die runs warm with the heater on.
  • Antenna pigtail: the U.FL end presses straight down onto the module's socket until it clicks; the SMA end screws onto the antenna through the box wall.
